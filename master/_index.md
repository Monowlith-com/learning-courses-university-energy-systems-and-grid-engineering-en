# Course overview – University · Energy Systems and Grid Engineering {#kurseinstieg}

This independent self-study course covers electric power and energy balances, network structures, equipment, generation, storage, control, power flow and system assessment. It assumes basic mathematics, complex numbers and introductory electrical engineering. It does not confer an academic degree or qualify anyone for high-voltage work. The course contains **7 modules and 21 lessons**, with worked examples, self-check exercises and advanced study sections.
